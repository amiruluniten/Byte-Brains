# T5 evidence — source-market segmentation (clusters named, tiers communicated)

Ticket: amiruluniten/Byte-Brains#6. Built TDD: `pipeline/tests/test_segmentation.py`
(17 tests, offline fixtures) written red first, then
`pipeline/src/bytebrains_pipeline/segmentation.py` + additive schema/wiring.

## What was built

- New bundle fragment `source_segmentation` in the data bundle (additive schema
  extension; `SCHEMA_VERSION` stays 1.0.0, no existing model changed).
- New module `pipeline/src/bytebrains_pipeline/segmentation.py`: WEF TTDI ingestion
  (SDMX/DATA360 shape: filters `COMP_BREAKDOWN_1=WEF_TTDI_VAL`, selects the latest
  `TIME_PERIOD` <= 2024 per indicator and REF_AREA), feature building, deterministic
  k-means, rule-based segment naming, quartile tiers.
- Additive wiring only: `emit.py` (new optional `wef_csv` param, default
  `source/dataset/WEF_TTDI.csv`; fragment + file sha256 added to sources/fragments),
  `validate.py` (`check_segmentation_reconciliation`), `bundle/models.py`
  (`SegmentationFragment`, `MarketSegment`, `ClusterProfile`, `FeatureDef`,
  `SegmentationSource`, closed segment-name vocabulary), `bundle/__init__.py` exports.
- Committed fixture `tests/fixtures/wef_ttdi.fixture.csv`, generated by
  `tests/fixtures/make_wef_fixture.py` (stdlib csv; slices the real WEF download to
  the 18 covered REF_AREA codes x 5 indicators + decoy rows the parser must filter).
- Feature subset and naming rationale documented in `pipeline/README.md`.

## Determinism / stability

- k-means with fixed seed 42, best of 12 consecutive seeded restarts (42-53,
  lowest inertia 53.48 for k=4); markets processed in sorted order.
- Tests assert: re-run produces an identical fragment JSON; cluster labels stable
  across runs; bundle-level fragments equal across re-runs (T4's rerun test also
  covers the new fragment).

## 2024 results (real data run, bundle checksum c20b2099...)

| segment | markets | mean yield | profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| High-Yield Long-Haul | Australia, Chinese Taipei, France, Germany, Japan, Russia, South Korea, United Kingdom, United States | RM5,371 | high wallet/development, lower volume |
| High-Growth Emerging | China, India, Indonesia | RM4,591 | +75.6% mean arrivals growth |
| Low-Yield Steady | Pakistan, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam | RM3,406 | below-median yield, flat growth |
| Volume Traps | Singapore, Brunei | RM1,675 | lowest yield, highest volume — the same-day regional traffic the arrivals KPI over-rewards |

Yield quartile boundaries (2024, 18 yield markets): q25 3,597.70 / q50 5,085.92 /
q75 5,393.03. Every market with a yield carries a tier; tiers and clusters derive
from the same `source_market` figures (validated on emit and on every reload).

## Missing-data handling (explicit, with attribution)

- Brunei, Chinese Taipei, Russia: absent from WEF TTDI 2024 -> all five `wef_*`
  features `None`, listed in `wef_missing`, median-imputed for clustering only.
- Myanmar: absent from WEF AND from the receipts table (no yield) -> excluded from
  clustering, reason stated.
- Bangladesh: arrivals-only (no yield); Canada, Netherlands: receipts-only (no
  volume) -> excluded with stated reasons.
