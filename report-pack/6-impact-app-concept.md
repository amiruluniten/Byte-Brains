# 6 · Impact & commercial — the app concept (hand-off for the report's Impact section)

This is the agreed product concept for the Impact & Commercial criterion (KOM1–KOM5).
The judges do not score a built product — they score how clearly the team explains the
implementation model (KOM4, KOM5). Write it as a model, not as shipping software.

## The one-paragraph pitch

An AI-first itinerary planner, positioned as the official channel under the Ministry of
Tourism ("Pelancongan"). The visitor states where they are from, where they are going,
and what experience they want (or asks the planner to customise). The planner matches
them to registered local businesses that fit the request. Free to register for both
sides. The platform earns a **per-booking commission from vendors** (Booking.com/Agoda
model) — not a payment-processing fee, which would need financial licensing.

## What the data says the product must fix (say this before the product)

- The stagnation line: 2024 real per-visitor yield equals 2019 (constant 2019 prices).
  Recovery was **extensive growth** (more visitors), not **intensive growth** (more
  value per visitor). The product attacks intensive growth.
- Target segment: **High-Growth Emerging** (China, Indonesia, India) — strongest
  arrivals growth (+75.6% mean), solid mid yield (RM4,591), first-time visitors who
  genuinely need discovery. This segment comes straight from the segmentation in
  `2-method-notes.md` §2.3 — the product is prescribed by the analysis, not bolted on.
- Not the target: **Volume Traps** (Singapore, Brunei). Same-day visitor traffic across
  the land border will not open a trip planner for a same-day visit. Say this
  explicitly — it shows the analysis drives the design.

## Evidence rule (protects you in Q&A)

Cite **tourism literature on digital trip planning** (studies linking online itinerary
planning to higher visitor expenditure and longer stays — which are exactly the two
parts of tourism yield). Do **not** cite vendor-level "businesses with an app earn
more" comparisons: that is selection bias, and vendor revenue is not national tourism
yield. One or two citations is enough.

## Scope: core now, integrations later

- **Core product (present this):** the AI itinerary planner matched to the yield
  segments — one focused product, one clear implementation model.
- **Roadmap / scalability (one slide or paragraph):** integration with transport
  (e.g. Grab), maps, and non-tourism services; expansion to more source markets.
  KOM4 explicitly rewards expansion potential ("berpotensi untuk dikembangkan").
- Do not pitch a government super-app. Focused scope scores (KDH2); super-app scope
  reads as unbuilt.

SDG 8.9 — sustainable tourism policies that create value and jobs — plus SDG 17.18:
better measurement (value per visitor, not arrival counts) enables better policy.
